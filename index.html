Customer information

Our policies & service terms

Please read these summaries before agreeing to a service or sale. The specific written quotation or sale agreement will confirm the terms that apply to your transaction.

1. Repair enquiries & quotations

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. We will agree the item, reported fault, inspection requirements, applicable fees and scope before starting work. Initial estimates may change if further faults are discovered; we will seek approval for additional work and costs.

If an item is uneconomical or cannot be repaired, we will explain the findings as far as we can establish them. Any inspection fee will be disclosed and agreed beforehand.

2. Collection & delivery

Collection and return delivery are subject to availability, location, item size, access and safe transport. Transport charges, timing, collection address and return arrangements must be agreed before dispatch.

Customers should disclose fragile, heavy, damaged or hazardous items and package items appropriately when instructed. We will discuss any special handling requirements before accepting an item.

3. Repair packages & payment

If a prepaid package is offered, the written description will state what is included, exclusions, transport charges, any assessment limits and how additional work is approved. We will not treat a payment as authorisation for undisclosed extras.

Payment methods and due dates will be stated in the quote or invoice.

4. Testing, results & limitations

Testing depends on the item, the reported fault, available equipment and the work agreed. We will describe the checks actually performed and any limitations we know about.

Repair cannot guarantee that every hidden or unrelated fault will be discovered. We will explain relevant risks and seek consent before proceeding with work that may carry a material risk of further damage.

5. Repair workmanship & warranties

Any specific workmanship warranty, duration, coverage and exclusions will be provided in writing. Do not assume that every repair has the same warranty or that a manufacturer's warranty remains valid.

Nothing in these terms is intended to remove rights that customers have under applicable South African law, including rights that may apply under the Consumer Protection Act.

6. Refurbished products, returns & defects

Product listings should state whether an item is used, refurbished, tested, incomplete or sold with known defects. Photographs, included accessories, known faults, price and applicable warranty terms should be disclosed before purchase.

Returns, remedies and warranty rights will be handled according to the transaction terms and applicable law. A used or refurbished item wiil not have any guarantee and no returns,as we wont know the exact details of its lifespan. Contact us witin 7 days if a purchased item has a problem.

7. Unwanted items & salvage ownership

A customer may ask us to assess an unwanted item, but that alone does not give us permission to keep, dismantle or sell it. Ownership transfers only after a clear agreement with a person authorised to transfer the item.

For items accepted as salvage, we will record the agreed transfer and whether any payment, collection fee or other condition applies. Items not accepted remain the owner's responsibility, subject to any separate written arrangement.

8. Uncollected repair items

Any collection deadline, storage charge or proposed action regarding an uncollected item must be disclosed in advance and handled in accordance with applicable law. We will not assume ownership merely because an item has not been collected, however items not collected within specified time period may result in storage fees, and thereafter resold to defray costs.

9. Privacy & customer information

We use information supplied in enquiries to respond, assess service requests, arrange agreed collections and manage transactions. We should only request information relevant to the service.

We will take reasonable steps to protect personal information and handle it in line with applicable privacy law, including POPIA.

10. Electronics disposal & recycling

Items that cannot reasonably be reused may require responsible electronic-waste handling. Acceptance depends on the item and our ability to handle it appropriately. We will not promise recycling services that have not been arranged.

11. Website & external links

This website provides information and contact options. External websites are independently operated, and their content, availability, prices and policies are not controlled by SP Electronics Online.

12. Complaints & resolving problems

Please contact us on WhatsApp with your name, transaction details and a description of the concern. We will review the matter and respond as reasonably as possible. Applicable legal rights and remedies remain unaffected.
