Simple Process

How it works

  1. Tell us about the item. Describe the appliance, its fault or why you no longer need it.
  2. Agree on the next step. We explain any assessment fees, repair quote or proposed transfer of ownership before proceeding.
  3. Inspect and test. Suitable items are assessed and tested to the extent described in the service or product listing.
  4. Repair, reuse or recycle. With the necessary customer consent, suitable items may be repaired, resold or sent for responsible recycling.

An item left for repair does not automatically become our property. We will obtain clear agreement before accepting an item for resale or disposal.
